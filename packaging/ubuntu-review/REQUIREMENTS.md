# Initial Ubuntu review checklist

Prepared: source/binary artifacts, GPL notice, build instructions, source build,
Lintian report, detector test, author desktop lifecycle verification.

Still requires review: current development series and GNOME compatibility;
maintainer identity/signatures; dependency and complete license/provenance audit;
privileged input reader/IPC security; GNOME lifecycle integration; clean-VM tests;
autopkgtest; ongoing maintainer commitment. Sponsorship is technical review and
upload assistance, not a financial contribution. Archive admission is separate.

The optional Ko-fi link has not been removed. Do not confuse it with technical
Ubuntu sponsorship. Do not rebuild the reviewed files just to remove a link;
discuss UI/distribution-policy concerns during initial review first.

Official guidance:
https://ubuntu.com/project/docs/how-ubuntu-is-made/processes/new-packages/
https://ubuntu.com/project/docs/contributors/uploading/find-a-sponsor/
